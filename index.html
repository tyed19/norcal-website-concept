The Practice

Most practices offer liposuction. This one is built around it — one surgeon, one specialty, and results measured in inches and definition, not appointments.

NorCal Liposculpture is a physician-led practice in Roseville devoted to body contouring: VASER® and high-definition liposculpture, Renuvion skin tightening, lipedema treatment, injectables, and medical weight loss.

Every consultation is with the doctor. Every procedure is performed by the doctor. That is the whole model — and it is why patients come from across Northern California.

Meet the Doctor

Jack Friedlander, M.D.

Liposculpture Specialist

Dr. Friedlander has built his practice around a single discipline: liposculpture performed at the highest level. He trained with Dr. John Millard, a pioneer of high-definition liposuction, and with Dr. Grant Hamlet — and today is asked to train other physicians in the technique himself.

Patients meet him at the consultation, see him at every follow-up, and can reach him after their procedure. That continuity is the practice's signature.
